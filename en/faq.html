Visitor FAQ

Practical answers for planning your visit. Verify specifics on the official site.

When is the exhibition open?expand_more
The exhibition runs July 16–19, 2026, 10:00–18:00 (the final day closes at 17:00). The conference runs July 15–19.
How do I register to visit?expand_more
Pre-register online and enter with your confirmation/badge. Check the official site for the registration window and any qualification requirements.
Can children under 12 enter?expand_more
For safety reasons, children under 12 are not permitted in the exhibition halls.
How do I get to the venue?expand_more
Take the metro to Taipei Nangang Exhibition Center station (Bannan/Wenhu lines), which connects directly to the venue. High Speed Rail and TRA arrive at nearby Nangang Station.
Is there parking?expand_more
Parking is available at and around the exhibition center, but public transit is strongly recommended during the event.
How can my company exhibit?expand_more
Contact the exhibition operator, Chan Chao International, at 02-2659-6000 or CustomerService@chanchao.com.tw.
How do I book partnering meetings?expand_more
Use the conference’s online partnering system to pre-book one-on-one meetings ahead of the event.
Do forums require separate registration?expand_more
Conference forums and symposia generally require registration, and some are paid. See the official programme for details.
